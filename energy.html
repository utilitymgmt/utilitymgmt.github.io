Don't Overlook Your Energy Bills

Many businesses think the only way to lower their electric and gas costs is to switch their utility company. While you can’t change your local utility provider, you can choose who supplies electricity and natural gas to your utility. Power continues to be delivered by the utility company, but businesses may be able to secure a lower rate for supply.
